## What is counted

- **Outcome**: every run is passed, handed over, failed (failed or died), waiting or running; landed afterwards are the handed-over runs whose work is on its target branch (read from git, never fetched) or that a person settled as landed.
- **Why**: a fixed category, never the recorded text: for a hand-over the leading keyword of each recorded reason, for a failure the kind of the failed step, or step-discipline, budget, usage-limit, died.
- **Attempts**: per step of the layer definition, from the step records; first attempt is the share of records that passed without a second attempt.
- **Review rounds**: counted for the runs that were reviewed.
- **A person acted**: one action for each of: the run was handed over, it waited at an approval step, it has a settlement record, it was started again by hand. Zero-touch runs passed and were shipped by the gate.
- **Time and cost**: median and total per workflow and class, over finished runs; dry runs are their own rows and have no cost; a real run without a recorded cost is counted under without cost.
- **Default branch**: runs whose work is on the layer's default branch: shipped by the gate, or handed over and landed afterwards.
- **Miss**: a batch failure bisected to a run whose check of that command passed below full depth; the miss rate is over the targeted runs batches verified, per command and per selection strategy.
- **Runs with a Proof plan**: runs whose plan ends with a Proof plan table; every other number of Reviews and proof counts only these runs.
- **Review rounds per run**: mean and max of the review rounds of the runs with a Proof plan that were reviewed.
- **Stopped early**: runs stopped before the build or before another review round (prove, evidence, a moved target, or rounds that made no progress), by reason and by the party who can clear it.
- **Repeated blockers**: blockers whose fingerprint (criterion or file, and kind) came back in a later round, by cause (fixable, needs-environment, needs-declaration, needs-person).
- **Idle hours**: time spent in review rounds whose verdict was the same as the round before.
- **Missing declarations**: the declarations stops asked a layer author for, most frequent first: add these first.
